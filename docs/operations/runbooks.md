# Operations runbooks

## Crisis dispatch
Preserve the critical event, route the user to emergency support and Safety Plan Step 6, and ensure the human triage queue receives the 15-minute SLA responsibility. Do not wait for an LLM or suppress a positive signal.

## Audit discrepancy
Stop destructive repair, preserve the append-only evidence, compare tamper-chain inputs, and escalate to the security/data owner.

## Data incident
Contain access, preserve evidence, follow the approved UAE incident process, and involve clinical/legal/security owners. This scaffold does not define a production notification procedure.
