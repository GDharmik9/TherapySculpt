# Design principles

1. Safety before convenience: Gate 2 overrides normal navigation.
2. Deterministic clinical math: pure, testable code; no LLM scoring.
3. Non-diagnostic language: screening supports discussion, not diagnosis.
4. Human-in-the-loop escalation: positive acute signals route to human review.
5. Privacy by default: granular consent and employer/insurer firewall.
6. UAE localization: do not export health data or compute outside approved boundaries.
7. Additive preservation: unused or superseded artifacts are kept aside, not deleted.
8. Bilingual and RTL-friendly UX: patient-facing content has English and Arabic variants.
